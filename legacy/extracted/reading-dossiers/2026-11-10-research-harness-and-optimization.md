**Consequence.** Start with the reconstruction for one fixed exact state measurement. The novel part, if any, must isolate an additional result on a stated model with a meaningful decision consequence. A generic hierarchy by information, an equivalent reformulation, or a change from feedback to a disturbance-prefix oracle has insufficient novelty evidence. Novelty of a general scheduling result, the usefulness of S2–S4, and any general result about affine rules remain unestablished in this review.

### Reproduction of the A4 open-loop baseline

A4 Example 1 maximizes the guaranteed terminal reward $x_2(T)$ with $x_1(T)$ constrained to $[2,10]$ and $T=120$. The printed coefficients have four decimal places. Propagating those coefficients as exact rationals gives **1.495710098124**. The benchmark script computes the terminal support sums, constructs a feasible control sequence, and verifies equality with a Lagrange upper bound using exact arithmetic. It certifies the value for the literal printed data. [A4, PDF p. 12](../../corpus/our_papers/A4_Дмитрук_2022.pdf#page=12).

A3 identifies the discretization step as $h=0.1$ and traces the example to A2. Starting from A2's continuous dynamics and keeping inputs constant on each interval gives the transition with diagonal entries $\cos(h)$ and off-diagonal entries $\sin(h),-\sin(h)$. The input column becomes $(1-\cos(h),\sin(h))$. Reconstructing these coefficients to the decimal precision pinned in the script gives **1.501101989382**, which rounds to the reported **1.501102**. This makes coefficient rounding a plausible explanation. The exact unrounded inputs used by the authors remain unconfirmed. For further replication, preserve both coefficient sets and label which one is used. [A2, PDF p. 15](../../corpus/our_papers/A2_Дмитрук_2018.pdf#page=15), [A3, PDF pp. 8–9](../../corpus/our_papers/A3_Костюкевич_Дмитрук_2021.pdf#page=8), [reproducer](../../specs/research-pilot/check_dmitruk_open_loop.py), [check record](../../artifacts/research-pilot/2026-11-10-pilot-checks.txt).

## 2. Research systems and factory practices

## 3. Scout output contract

For every source selected for the next question, record DOI or arXiv version, role (direct, adjacent, negative case, or method), available PDF and digest, page/section, precise claim and hypotheses, what changed in the question, unresolved overlap, and next action. Save the query and date even when the screen returns nothing. A public repository is an index of candidates until a pinned revision, license, relevant module, and reproduction are recorded. Keep terms in English and retain original quotations as source data. A source with abstract-only status stays out of the numerical and formal evidence paths.
