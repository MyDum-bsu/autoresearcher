## Meaning of success, fixed before new work

The target is a substantive result in the approved OGU/ARO information/policy bridge with:

1. An exact model, observation timing, admissible policy class, domains, constraints, objective and quantifiers.
2. A concrete mathematical contribution: a theorem with checked proof, a certified separating/impossibility construction addressing an actual literature gap, or a rigorously justified method with a declared scope. Merely reconstructing coordinate-box intersection or elementary rank-one reasoning does not meet this goal.
3. Direct primary-source comparison showing precisely how the result differs from the closest inspected results; no unresolved decisive source or known stronger result silently omitted. Search silence cannot certify novelty. The honest output can be a **candidate contribution with a documented literature delta**, not a universal priority guarantee.
4. Executed independent exact/formal checks covering the actual claim, positive and invalid controls, and sound evidence binding. General theorems cannot be accepted from finite examples. Written arguments without a checker remain proposals.
5. A useful interpretation for the original control/information question and a reproducible dossier without unresolved blocking scientific or evidence-integrity defects.

Meeting these conditions produces a candidate ready for owner scientific review. Human novelty/significance judgment and attestation remain human; no workflow or LLM can award them. The coordinator must inspect the evidence before reporting success. Green engineering tests, model agreement, convincing prose, familiar lemmas or a completed workflow are not success.
